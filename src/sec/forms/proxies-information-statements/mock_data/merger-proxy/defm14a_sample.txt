<h1>The Business Combination</h1>
<p>
Merge SPAC Inc., a blank check company, has entered into a business combination
with Acme Target Inc., a leading operating company in its sector. Upon the closing
of the business combination with Acme Target Inc., the post-combination company
will continue the business of Acme Target Inc.
</p>
<h1>PIPE Financing</h1>
<p>
In connection with the business combination, certain institutional investors have
agreed to purchase, in a private placement, shares for an aggregate PIPE investment
of $150,000,000, to be funded immediately prior to the closing of the business
combination.
</p>
